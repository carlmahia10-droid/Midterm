Ordering Made Easy

Step 1

Select Your Treats

Head over to our Order/Contact page. Mix and match your favorites like our fudgy Chocolate Crinkles, premium Cream Cheese Cookies, or pick a custom cake layer size. And our estimator to calculate your estimate bll.

Step 2

Customize Your Design

If you're ordering a cake, choose your base flavor (like our crowd-favorite Ube) and your preferred icing style. Don't forget to use the description box to tell us about your dream themes, colors!

Step 3

Submit Your Request

Fill out your name, email, and choose your order type (Daily Pickup, Event Catering, or ...Custom Request). Click Send Bakery Request to send your inquiry straight to our kitchen.

Step 4

Order Verification

Be ready for our response for your order and be excite on whats coming, our shop will review your order. We will contact you via email or text within a few hours to confirm details,pick pickup slot, and send down payment instructions.
