About Our Bakery

Our Story

Baked fresh daily with love and local ingredients.

Welcome to Ley's Sweet Treats! What started out as a passionate kitchen experiment has bloomed into a dedicated home bakery. We believe that every celebration—no matter how big or small—deserves a memorable centerpiece.

From our pillowy-soft cookies and fudgy crinkles to our beautifully detailed custom multi-layer cakes, every single order is treated as an art piece. We strictly bake to order, ensuring that your custom treats arrive on your table perfectly moist, aromatic, and spectacularly fresh.

Thank you for letting us sweeten your special moments!
